Our story

Since 2010 · Beirut

Some people leave their country and never look back. Ahmad El Assaad wasn't one of them.

After spending years building a life and career in Europe, he returned to Lebanon with a simple idea: to build homes that he himself would want to live in.

That idea became Pride Invests.

We don't believe a home is only defined by its size or luxury. It's about thoughtful layouts, quality materials, natural light, outdoor space, and the confidence that every detail has been considered. They are the things that make a home unique.

Every project we undertake is designed with the same approach: build carefully, avoid shortcuts, and focus on quality that lasts beyond handover.

Today, as we continue to grow in Lebanon, we're also preparing to bring that same philosophy to Portugal and France.

Our goal has never been to build the most projects. It's to build homes people are proud to own and love living in them for years to come.

Ahmad El Assaad Founder, Pride Invests
