An affordable gateway to luxury

Located a few minutes from Beirut, Pride of Martakla 2 is an affordable gateway to luxury.

From the marble reception in every apartment to the panoramic elevators, every detail of this project has been carefully selected to meet the highest standards possible.

Gallery

Built, delivered, lived in

Specifications

Selected to the highest standard

Structure

Reinforced concrete structure — foundations, retaining walls, shear walls, columns and slabs — engineered to zone II-B seismic design.

Envelope

Double external walls (25 cm) faced in natural stone and WPC, waterproofed and thermally insulated roofing, aluminum openings with bronze double glazing, electrical rolling shutters.

Elevators

Two panoramic elevators with variable voltage and frequency, cabins with stainless-steel telescopic doors.

Parking

Mechanical ventilation system, pavé stone finish, electrical parking gate with remote control.

Electricity

Generator with electrical transfer switch and fuel tank, 220/380 V three-phase with grounding, TV satellite and cable provisions, videophone and interphone, emergency lighting in parking and common areas.

Water

Water tanks and pumping systems for domestic and potable water.

Flooring

Marble in the reception areas; high-quality ceramic tiles throughout the remaining spaces.

Doors & paint

Massive oak door frames with oak-veneer leaves on MDF, finished in quality paint.

Plumbing

Top European PPR piping, wall-hung sanitary fixtures, jacuzzi in the duplexes.

Kitchens

Cabinetry in wood veneer on MDF with marble countertops.

Climate

Heating system provided; air-conditioning infrastructure in place. Differential protection with standard plugs and outlets.
