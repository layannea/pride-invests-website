A village of your own

Eighteen low-rise blocks step down a wooded hillside in Louayzeh, a gated community where children play safely and every apartment opens to green. Choose a garden apartment, a first floor home, a duplex or a roof duplex, from 175 to 390 m² with three to five master bedrooms.

Ownership is designed to be within reach: 15% down, with the remainder in moderate monthly installments. A 175 m² apartment, for example, comes to USD 629 per month over six years.
