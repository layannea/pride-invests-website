A dream come true on the hills of Yarzeh

Nestled on one of the most beautiful hills of Yarzeh in the Baabda area, The Yarzeh Townhouses offers an exquisite lifestyle with fast, easy access to Beirut. With beautiful surroundings and breathtaking views, it is surely a dream come true.

The Yarzeh Townhouses is a combination of modern and traditional architecture: six townhouses embedded in two mansions, Mansion Redwood and Mansion Magnolia. Each townhouse has its own private garden and its own unique character, and each mansion keeps a swimming pool, a jacuzzi, a tanning area, a gym, a sauna and a steam room.

Every part of this project is executed with premium quality materials and very high-end finishing, down to the smallest details. We invite you to experience The Yarzeh Townhouses — a journey into the most distinguished mansions in Yarzeh.

Specifications

Finished to the smallest detail

Structure & masonry

Seismic-design concrete structure with external double walls.

Facades & openings

Traditional Egyptian stone with natural beige stone cladding, steel balustrades, PVC windows, electric shutters and Parklex coating.

Waterproofing & insulation

Membrane waterproofing on all exposed and wet areas and flower beds, with thermal insulation.

Water supply

Underground common water tank plus an individual rooftop tank per townhouse.

Flooring

Cut-to-size first-choice marble in reception areas, solid parquet in kitchens and the main master bedroom, engineered parquet in the other masters, ceramic and marble in the bathrooms.

Ceilings & woodwork

Water-resistant gypsum false ceilings in receptions, bedrooms and corridors; doors on solid wood frames; Egger-foiled kitchen cabinetry.

Electrical & smart home

European wiring devices and videophone, Schneider panel boards, Liban Cables wiring, EIB home automation linked to phones and tablets — AC, floor heating, lighting, dimming and shutters — plus CCTV monitored from the guard room, provision for TV distribution, and FUJI lifts with custom internal lifts.

Mechanical

Individual central floor heating for the entire space, silent central air conditioning throughout, and a custom-made elevator in each townhouse.

Sanitary

First-choice European fixtures — Villeroy & Boch or Duravit — with German Hansgrohe fittings.

Security

Automatic parking gate with remote control, EIB smart system linked to phones, and 24/7 monitored CCTV.

Signature features

Three to five underground parkings and a private driver's quarter per townhouse, a 20 m² basement storage room, private gardens, a custom fireplace in each home, exquisite kitchens, walk-in closets, a sound system in the main master shower — and per mansion, a shared 10 × 4 m pool, jacuzzi, tanning area, gym, sauna and steam room.
