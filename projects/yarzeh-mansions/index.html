Ten minutes from Beirut, a world away

On the hills of Yarzeh in Baabda, two stone mansions — Daisy and Jasmine — hold four private residences of 445 to 690 m², with views running from the Mediterranean to the ridges of Mount Lebanon.

Each mansion keeps a pool and sun deck, a fitness room and sauna, four underground parking spaces, generous storage and a fireplace, with a KNX smart system quietly running heating, cooling and hot water.
